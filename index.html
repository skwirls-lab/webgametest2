HOW TO PLAY

THE BASICS

You are locked to the glowing band at the bottom. Shoot the centipede before it reaches you. Hit a middle segment and the swarm splits — every split grows a new head, and heads move faster.

MUSHROOMS

Four shots clear a mushroom. They block you and the swarm, so the field is your terrain. Poisoned mushrooms make segments dive straight down — clear them fast.

COMBO

Chain kills within the combo window to build up to x8 score. Getting hit resets it. Big multipliers are where the leaderboard runs live.

SURGE

Kills charge the SURGE meter. Fire it with Shift for six seconds of double score, piercing rounds, faster fire and slowed enemies.

DASH

Space dashes you a short distance and makes you briefly invincible. It is the answer to spiders, fleas and queen volleys.

THE HIVE QUEEN

Every fifth wave is a boss. Her body armours her — you have to land shots on her head. She gets faster and angrier in each of her three phases.

BESTIARY

  • Spider — zigzags through your band and eats mushrooms. Worth more the closer it is when it dies.
  • Flea — drops when your band gets too clear and plants fresh mushrooms. Takes two shots and speeds up after the first.
  • Scorpion — poisons every mushroom it walks past. Highest bounty on the board.
